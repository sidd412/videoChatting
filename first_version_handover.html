🎨 1. Look, Feel & Target Audience

Talksy has been designed from the ground up as a premium, highly engaging platform catering to modern mobile users.

  • The Visual Style: The app utilizes a premium "Liquid Glass" (glassmorphism) design theme. Layouts are constructed with semi-transparent frosted panels, vibrant neon gradient buttons, and smooth micro-animations. With high-contrast typography in Nunito Sans and custom system navigation bar blending, the UI feels native, premium, and unified in both dark and light modes.
  • Target Audience: The primary target market consists of **Gen-Z and Millennials (18-35 age bracket)**. These users look for instant, zero-latency social connections, interactive matching filters, and a secure environment to make calls worldwide.

🌐 2. App Components & Tech Stack (Simple Breakdown)

Talksy's architecture consists of three main components that communicate securely in the cloud to provide a smooth user experience:

📱 2.1 The Front-End (Android Application)

Built using Google's modern Kotlin & Jetpack Compose framework. It handles all live interfaces, video channels, client chat UI, and payment callbacks. This layout uses very low battery and ensures fluid transitions.

🖥️ 2.2 The Engine (Backend Server)

The central processor built with Node.js & Socket.io and hosted on Render. It processes user credentials, manages matching filters, tracks wallet status in real-time, and operates the active call signaling.

💾 2.3 Caching & Database (Redis & MongoDB)

We use MongoDB to permanently save user profile configurations, blocked lists, and message history. We integrated Redis (in-memory database) which acts as the matchmaker's quick-access memory, finding and pairing active users globally in milliseconds.

✅ 3. Completed & Working Features (V1 Base)

  • Instant Live Video Calling: Powered by **Agora RTC**, providing crystal-clear HD video and audio channels that adjust bandwidth consumption dynamically based on network conditions to prevent call drops.
  • Real-Time Chatting: Direct text messaging powered by WebSockets, including online status indicators, read receipts, and permanent history storage.
  • Intelligent Match Preferences: Real-time filtering by age limits, gender preferences, and regional boundaries.
  • Coin-Based Calling: Call duration checks and wallet management. The backend checks coin balance before pairing and automatically deducts coins every minute of the call, cleanly disconnecting if the balance runs out.
  • Secure Payment Checkout: Payment integration via **Juspay Gateway** with secure web redirect checkout, allowing UPI, debit cards, and net banking transactions to instantly credit the user's coin balance.

🌐 4. The User Journey Map (Step-by-Step)

This outlines the user's progress through the app, from the first launch to a recurring call and transaction cycle:

🚪 4.1 Onboarding & Setup

  • User launches the app and views the onboarding introduction (carousel cards).
  • Instantly registers via Google Sign-In or uses **Guest Login** to trial the app.
  • Saves basic profile details (Name, Age, Gender, Country) and instantly receives **100 free coins** as a sign-up incentive.

🎯 4.2 Matching Loop (Discovery Screen)

  • User configures their search preferences (target age, gender, and regional filter).
  • Taps **"Find Match"** to enter the Redis queue.
  • The system pairs them with a live online user and initiates a secure, high-definition **Agora Video Call**.
  • During the call, they can:
      • Like (Heart icon): Save as a potential contact.
      • Add (Friend icon): Add to direct contact list.
      • Next/Skip: Taps next to instantly matching with another person.

💬 4.3 Chatting & Connections

  • If both users add each other during the video loop, they appear in each other's **Chats list**.
  • They can exchange private text messages, see online indicators, and continue the conversation without matchmaking.

💳 4.4 Coin Consumption & Wallet Recharge

  • Active call time consumes 1 coin per minute.
  • If coins drop below 10, a friendly warning prompts them to recharge.
  • User navigates to the **Wallet**, selects a coin package, and buys instantly via UPI or card using Juspay checkout, adding coins to their balance to continue calling.

⚠️ 8. Critical External Dependencies & Risks

📌 Google Play Store Approvals & Rejections

Publishing a video matchmaking app involves strict external factors that are out of our direct control:

  • Review Duration: Google's initial review process for new developer accounts typically takes **7 to 14 days** (and can sometimes extend up to 21 days for video/social apps).
  • Initial Rejections: Because Talksy falls under user-generated video calling, Google has strict policies regarding User Content Moderation (UGC). It is very common for new apps of this kind to get initially rejected or requested for compliance modifications. If this occurs, we will need to quickly add content reporting, blocking, and policy updates, which may cause minor delays in our launch targets.

🛡️ 9. Compliance, Content Moderation & User Safety

To comply with global privacy laws and protect our users, several moderation safeguards are integrated directly into the system architecture:

  • Immediate Block & Report: Users can block or report abusive partners during any live match or chat. Once blocked, the matched partner is instantly disconnected and permanently prevented from being matched with that user again.
  • Peer-to-Peer Video Privacy: All video streams are routed peer-to-peer (P2P) using Agora’s encrypted communication nodes. Video streams never pass through our backend server, which ensures 100% privacy and protects us from video intercept liabilities.
  • Temporary Location Handling: User location (latitude/longitude) is parsed locally on the device to determine match filters and is never permanently stored on our databases, keeping us fully compliant with data privacy laws.
